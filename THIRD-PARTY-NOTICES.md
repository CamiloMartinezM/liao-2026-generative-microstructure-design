## Design of the theme

The theme in `presentation/beamerthemetalk.sty` adapts design elements of [TU Delft presentation](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/tu-delft-presentation/jcxrmnxssdst) by Novanext, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): its plain square blocks, the logo in the footer, and its section dividers, a centred box over a band. The theme draws them in its own code, with its own colours, fonts, footer contents and divider band, and without the TU Delft colours, fonts or logos. Its headline of section names with a dot per frame below each follows Beamer's Frankfurt theme, part of the [beamer](https://ctan.org/pkg/beamer) class, distributed under the LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c or the GNU General Public License version 2 or later.
